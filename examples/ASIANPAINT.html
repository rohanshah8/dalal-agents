Business overview

Asian Paints is an 80+ year old company and the largest home decor player in India [E1]. Its brands include Asian Paints and Apco, and its portfolio spans: - wall paints, wall coverings, waterproofing and textures; - adhesives and mechanised tools; - modular kitchens, sanitaryware, lighting, soft furnishings and uPVC windows [E1].

It also has industrial coatings (PPGAP JV) and international businesses [E65][E53]. Management expects industrial coatings to grow faster than decorative [E49]. FY26 revenue of ₹35,584 cr is roughly three times Berger Paints' ₹11,880 cr [F10][F143].

Distribution and home decor reach: - More than 6,000 retail touchpoints were added in FY26 [E83]. - Home decor is sold through 74 Beautiful Home Stores across 20 states [E87].

Backward integration is under way. Commercial production has begun at the Dahej VAE plant, with 1,50,000 tpa capacity for captive use [E9]. A white cement plant in Fujairah, UAE is fully operational [E71]. New products contribute 17% of revenues [E51].
